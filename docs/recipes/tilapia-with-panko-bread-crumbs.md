# Tilapia with Panko Bread Crumbs

**Cook:** 12 min

## Ingredients

- Tilapia
- Panko bread crumbs
- Parmesan cheese
- Garlic salt
- Oregano
- Mayo
- Lemon juice

## Instructions

1. Mix together panko bread crumbs, Parmesan cheese, garlic salt, and oregano.
2. Dredge tilapia in bread crumb mixture.
3. Bake for 12 minutes.
4. Top tilapia with mayo, lemon juice, and Parmesan cheese.
