# Baked Hummus

**Cook:** 15-20 min

## Ingredients

- 27 oz plain hummus
- 1/4 cup crumbled feta cheese
- 1/3 cup kalamata olives
- 1/3 cup fresh tomatoes
- 2 TBS olive oil

## Instructions

1. Spread hummus evenly into an 8x8 inch square baking dish.
2. Sprinkle with feta cheese, being sure to evenly cover the surface.
3. In a separate bowl, gently toss tomatoes and olives together until well blended.
4. Layer the olive and tomato mixture on top of feta cheese.
5. Drizzle with 2 TBS olive oil.
6. Bake at 350°F for 15-20 minutes.
7. Serve with pita chips or crackers.
