# 10 Ravioli Bake

## Ingredients

- Marinara sauce
- Ravioli
- Mozzarella cheese
- Parmesan cheese

## Instructions

1. Spread marinara sauce on the bottom of the pan.
2. Add a layer of ravioli.
3. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese on top.
4. Repeat layers.
5. Add Parmesan and mozzarella on top.
6. Bake.
