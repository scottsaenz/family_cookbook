# Sauteed Squash with Tomatoes and Peas

## Ingredients

- squash
- butter
- sliced tomatoes
- peas

## Instructions

1. Saute squash in butter.
2. Add sliced tomatoes and peas.
3. Cover until done.
4. Then cover off until juice is absorbed.
