# Mini Tortilla Crust Pizza

## Ingredients

- Tortilla crust
- Sauce or shells
- Cheese
- Pepperoni

## Instructions

1. Cut tortilla crust to fit muffin pan.
2. Press in pan.
3. Put sauce or shell.
4. Put cheese on top and pepperoni.
5. Bake until done.
