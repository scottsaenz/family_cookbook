# Ultimate Party Meatballs

**Cook:** 15 minutes | **Servings:** 30 appetizer servings

## Ingredients

- 1 14-ounce can Ocean Spray Jellied Cranberry Sauce
- 1 12-ounce bottle Heinz Chili Sauce
- 1 2-pound bag frozen, pre-cooked, cocktail-size meatballs

## Instructions

1. Combine sauces in a large saucepan.
2. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring until smooth.
3. Add meatballs.
4. Cover and cook for 15 minutes or until meatballs are heated through, stirring occasionally.

## Notes

Combine the robust taste of Heinz Chili Sauce and the tangy taste of Ocean Spray Jellied Cranberry Sauce for a uniquely satisfying flavor guaranteed to be a hit!
