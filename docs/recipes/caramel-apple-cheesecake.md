# Caramel Apple Cheesecake

**Cook:** 35 min | **Servings:** 15

## Ingredients

- 1 can lucky leaf apple pie filling (21 oz)
- 1 graham cracker crust
- 2 pkgs cream cheese, softened
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/4 tsp vanilla
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup caramel topping
- 12 pecan halves
- 2 TBS chopped pecans

## Instructions

1. Reserve 3/4 c. pie filling. Spoon remaining filling into crust.
2. Blend cream cheese, sugar, and vanilla. Beat in eggs. Pour over pie filling in crust.
3. Bake at 350°F for 35 min or until center is set. Cool.
4. Meanwhile, combine reserved pie filling and caramel topping. Heat to soften.
5. Spoon evenly over top, spreading to within 1 inch of crust edge.
6. Arrange pecan halves around outer edge and sprinkle chopped nuts over center.
7. Serve 15.
