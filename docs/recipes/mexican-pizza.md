# Mexican Pizza

**Cook:** 8-10 min

## Ingredients

- Brown both sides of tortilla shell
- 1/2 cup refried beans
- Shell or top
- 2 1/2 tbsp red enchilada sauce
- Tomato
- Cheese
- Avocado

## Instructions

1. Brown both sides of the tortilla shell.
2. Spread 1/2 cup refried beans on the shell or top.
3. Add 2 1/2 tbsp red enchilada sauce.
4. Top with tomato, cheese, and avocado.
5. Bake at 400°F for 8-10 minutes.
