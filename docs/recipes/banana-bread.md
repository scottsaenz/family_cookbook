# Banana Bread

**Cook:** 1h

## Ingredients

- 1/3 cup vegetable oil
- 3 large mashed ripe bananas
- 1/2 tsp vanilla
- 3 eggs
- 2 2/3 cups Bisquick
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup walnuts

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Grease bottom of pan 9x3.
3. Stir all ingredients with fork until moistened.
4. Beat vigorously 1 minute.
