# Paula Deen Oven Fried Chicken

**Cook:** 40-50 minutes

## Ingredients

- boneless chicken
- salt & pepper
- 3/4 cup buttermilk
- 1/2 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1/2 tsp hot sauce
- 1 cup self-rising flour

## Instructions

1. Mix together buttermilk, mustard, garlic, and hot sauce.
2. Marinate chicken 2-8 hours.
3. Spray pan with oil.
4. Salt & pepper chicken.
5. Shake in plastic bag with flour.
6. Bake on cooling rack.
