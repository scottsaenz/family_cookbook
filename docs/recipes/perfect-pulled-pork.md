# Perfect Pulled Pork

**Servings:** 16-20

## Ingredients

- 5 lbs. boneless pork butt shoulder
- 1 1/2 tsp. smoked paprika
- 2 tsp. black pepper
- 1 tsp. cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp. dried thyme
- 1 tsp. garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 1 c. water
- soft sandwich buns

## Instructions

1. Combine all seasonings in a small bowl and evenly coat roast.
2. Place meat in a 5-quart slow cooker.
3. Add water, cover, and cook on LOW for 8-10 hours or on HIGH for 4-5 hours or until pork is very tender.
4. Place pork on large cutting board or platter and let rest for 10-15 minutes.
5. Pull, slice or chop to serve.
6. Serve in buns with barbecue sauce.
