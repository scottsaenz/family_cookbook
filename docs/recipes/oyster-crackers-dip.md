# Oyster Crackers Dip

**Cook:** 30 min

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup vegetable or canola oil or 2 cups vegetable oil
- 1 pkg Hidden Valley ranch dressing or 2 pkgs Hidden Valley ranch
- 1 TBS dill weed or 4 TBS dill weed
- 2 pkgs oyster crackers

## Instructions

1. Bake at 250°F for 30 minutes.
2. Stir every 10 minutes.
