# Barbs Cream Puffs

## Ingredients

- 1 stick butter
- 1 cup water
- 1 cup reg flour
- 4 eggs - beat 1 at a time
- 2 small instant pudding
- milk
- cream cheese
- cool whip

## Instructions

1. Put 1 tbsp in mounds on cookie sheet or spread batter in a 9x12 pan.
2. Cool mixture.
3. Add mixture on top - drizzle with chocolate then add cool whip after pudding.
4. Beat cream cheese.
5. Mix pudding with 1 1/4 cup milk.
6. Pour into softened cream cheese.
7. Add fruit and a little cool whip.
