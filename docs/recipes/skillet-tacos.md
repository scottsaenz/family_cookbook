# Skillet Tacos

**Servings:** 6

## Ingredients

- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 small red onion, chopped
- 1 can (15 ounces) whole kernel corn, drained
- 10 corn tortillas (6 inches), cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 (8-ounce) bottle taco sauce
- 1 1/4 cups shredded cheddar cheese, divided
- Hot pepper sauce, optional

## Instructions

1. In a large skillet, cook beef and onion over medium heat until meat is no longer pink; drain.
2. Add the corn, tortillas, taco sauce and 1 cup cheese; heat through.
3. Top with remaining cheese and sprinkle with hot sauce if desired.
