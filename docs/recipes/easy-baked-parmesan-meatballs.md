# Easy Baked Parmesan Meatballs

**Prep:** 15 minutes | **Total:** 35 minutes | **Servings:** 6 servings, 2 meatballs each

## Ingredients

- 1 lb. ground beef
- 1/2 cup KRAFT 100% Grated Parmesan Cheese
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
- 1 egg
- 1 clove garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. PREHEAT oven to 375°F.
2. Mix meat, cheese, parsley, egg and garlic.
3. SHAPE into 12 meatballs.
4. Place in foil-lined 15x10x1-inch baking pan.
5. BAKE 25 minutes or until cooked through.

## Notes

South Beach Diet Tip: Prepare as directed, substituting ground turkey for the ground beef.
