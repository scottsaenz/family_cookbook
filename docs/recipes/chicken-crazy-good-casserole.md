# Chicken Crazy Good Casserole

## Ingredients

- chicken cooker
- 6 strips bacon
- 2 cans chicken soup
- 2 cups Monterey Jack cheese
- 1 box spiral pasta
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Cook and crumble bacon.
2. Cut chicken into bite-size pieces.
3. Cook chicken in bacon drippings, add garlic, salt, and pepper.
4. Spray 9x13 pan.
5. Drain pasta, return to pot.
6. Add chicken, soup, and 1 cup of cheese, stir mix well.
7. Put in pan.
8. Top with bacon and cheese.
