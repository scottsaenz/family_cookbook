# Reduced Fat Lasagna

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lb lean ground beef
- 1 container reduced fat cottage cheese
- 1 container reduced fat parmesan cheese
- 8 oz reduced fat mozzarella cheese
- 1 jar spaghetti sauce
- 1 box lasagna noodles

## Instructions

1. Brown meat and drain.
2. Preheat oven to 375°F.
3. Layer cooked meat, sauce, and cheeses.
4. Repeat layers.
5. Bake for 45 minutes.
6. Let stand for 10 minutes before serving.
