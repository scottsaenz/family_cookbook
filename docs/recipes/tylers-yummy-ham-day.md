# Tylers Yummy Ham Day

**Cook:** 30-40 mins

## Ingredients

- 1 box pasta elbows, rigatoni, or ziti (cook according to package)
- 1 package fresh broccoli, steamed
- 1 12-16 oz package diced ham
- 2 cups cheddar cheese
- 2 cups mozzarella cheese
- 6 tbsp butter
- 1/2 cup sour cream

## Instructions

1. Mix all ingredients together except 1/2 cup mozzarella cheese and 1/2 cup cheddar cheese.
2. Place in baking dish.
3. Top with reserved cheese.
4. Bake at 375°F for 30-40 minutes.
