# Cream Cheese Crescent Rolls

**Cook:** 20-30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 cans Pillsbury butter crescent rolls
- 2 8 oz pkgs cream cheese, softened
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1/4 cup melted butter
- cinnamon
- sugar

## Instructions

1. Bake at 350°F for 20-30 minutes.
2. Unroll and spread 1 can of rolls on bottom of ungreased pan.
3. Combine cream cheese, sugar, and vanilla. Spread over rolls.
4. Spread other roll on top and spread over top melted butter, cinnamon, and sugar.
