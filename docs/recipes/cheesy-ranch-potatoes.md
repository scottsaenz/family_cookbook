# Cheesy Ranch Potatoes

**Cook:** 45-60 min

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs potatoes
- 16 oz sour cream
- 2 cups cheddar cheese
- 2 oz dry ranch dressing mix
- 2 oz real bacon bits

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Combine all ingredients in a large bowl.
3. Mix well.
4. Transfer to a baking dish.
5. Bake for 45-60 minutes.
