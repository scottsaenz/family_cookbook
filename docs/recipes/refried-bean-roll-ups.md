# Refried Bean Roll-Ups

**Prep:** 15 min | **Total:** 15 min | **Servings:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 can (16 oz) Old El Paso refried beans
- 1/2 cup Old El Paso salsa (any variety)
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 8 Old El Paso flour tortillas for burritos (8")
- 2 cups shredded lettuce
- 1/2 cup shredded Monterey Jack cheese (2 oz)

## Instructions

1. In 1-qt saucepan, heat beans, salsa and chili powder over medium heat about 5 min, stirring occasionally, until warm.
2. Spoon about 1/4 cup of the bean mixture onto center of each tortilla; spread slightly. Top with lettuce and cheese.
3. Fold in top and bottom of each tortilla; roll up. Serve with additional salsa if desired.

## Notes

Meat Lovers Roll-Ups: Super Easy Chicken - Heat Old El Paso refrigerated taco sauce with seasoned shredded chicken. Spoon 1/4 cup down center of beans. Speedy Ground Beef - Cook Old El Paso taco seasoning mix with ground beef as the package directs. Spoon 1/4 cup mixture down center of beans.
