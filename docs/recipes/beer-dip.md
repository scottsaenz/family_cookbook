# Beer dip

## Ingredients

- 2 (8-ounce) packages of softened cream cheese
- 1 (7-ounce) bag of shredded cheddar cheese
- 1 packet powdered Ranch dressing mix
- 6 ounces of beer

## Instructions

1. In a medium-sized bowl, mix all ingredients.
2. Blend well with a beater and chill for at least 1 hour.

## Notes

Serve with sturdy pretzel rods.
