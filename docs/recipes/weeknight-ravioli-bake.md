# weeknight ravioli bake

**Prep:** 10 min | **Total:** 55 min | **Servings:** 8 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 jar (26 oz.) spaghetti sauce
- 1 can (14½ oz.) diced tomatoes, undrained
- ½ cup water
- 2 pkg. (1 lb. each) frozen cheese ravioli
- 1 pkg. (7 oz.) Kraft 2% Milk Shredded Italian* Three Cheese Blend
- 2 Tbsp. Kraft Grated Parmesan Cheese

## Instructions

1. HEAT oven to 400°F.
2. MIX spaghetti sauce, tomatoes and water; spoon 1 cup onto bottom of 13x9-inch baking dish.
3. LAYER half the ravioli and 1 cup shredded cheese over sauce mixture. Top with remaining ravioli and sauce mixture. Sprinkle with remaining shredded cheese; cover.
4. BAKE 30 min.; uncover. Bake 15 min. or until ravioli is tender and heated through. Sprinkle with Parmesan. Let stand 10 min.

## Notes

Made with quality cheeses crafted in the USA.
