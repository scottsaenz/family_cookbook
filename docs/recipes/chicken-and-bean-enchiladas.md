# Chicken and Bean Enchiladas

## Ingredients

- chicken
- refried beans
- salsa
- cheese
- tortillas
- sauce

## Instructions

1. Combine chicken, refried beans, salsa, and cheese.
2. Soften tortillas.
3. Fill tortillas with mixture.
4. Roll and put in pan.
5. Top with sauce and cheese.
