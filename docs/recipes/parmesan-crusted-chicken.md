# parmesan-crusted chicken

**Cook:** 15-20 minutes | **Servings:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup Hellmann's Real Mayonnaise
- 1/4 cup Publix Grated Parmesan Cheese
- 1 3/4 lb Perdue Fit & Easy Boneless Chicken Breasts
- 4 teaspoons Italian bread crumbs

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Combine mayonnaise and cheese.
2. Arrange chicken on baking sheet; top evenly with mayonnaise mixture and sprinkle with bread crumbs.
3. Bake 15-20 minutes or until chicken is 165°F. Serve.
