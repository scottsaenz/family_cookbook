# Flourless Bean Chocolate Cake

**Cook:** 45 to 50 minutes | **Servings:** 6 to 8 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 3/4 cups cooked beans
- 1 1/2 cups semisweet chocolate chips or baking beans, melted and cooled
- 4 eggs
- 3/4 cup white sugar
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tbsp flaxseed, ground, for dusting

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 325°F.
2. Grease a 9-inch square pan, dust with flaxseed.
3. In a small bowl, mix beans and eggs, then sprinkle on top of the mixture in pan.
4. Bake 45 to 50 minutes.
5. Serve warm with whipped cream, if desired.
