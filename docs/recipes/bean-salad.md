# Bean Salad

## Ingredients

- Kidney beans
- Lima beans
- Green peppers
- Pimentos
- Onions
- Celery
- Chickpeas
- Yellow wax beans
- 1 1/2 cups white vinegar
- 1/2 cup water
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 tablespoon salt

## Instructions

1. Bring to boil: 1 1/2 cups white vinegar, 1/2 cup water, 2 cups sugar, 1 tablespoon salt.
2. Let cool to lukewarm.
3. Pour over beans.
4. Set overnight.
