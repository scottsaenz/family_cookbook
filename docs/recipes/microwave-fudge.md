# Microwave Fudge

**Cook:** 1 minute

## Ingredients

- 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
- 3 cups dark chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Microwave and mix for 1 minute.
2. Line a pan with nonstick wrap.
3. Pour mixture into pan and refrigerate for 2 hours.
