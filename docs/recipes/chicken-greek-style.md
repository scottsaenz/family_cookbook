# Chicken Greek Style

## Ingredients

- 2 chicken breasts
- 1 green pepper
- 1 onion
- 1 can tomatoes
- Feta cheese

## Instructions

1. Saute the green pepper and onion.
2. Add one can of tomatoes.
3. Season.
4. Add chicken, cooked.
5. Put over rice and add feta cheese on top.
