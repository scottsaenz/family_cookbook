# Guacamole-Salsa Salad

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, coarsely chopped
- 3 medium avocados
- Juice of 1 lime
- 3/4 cup Deli mild salsa
- 1 (8-oz) bag shredded lettuce

## Instructions

1. Chop cilantro and cut avocados into bite-size pieces; place both in medium bowl.
2. Squeeze lime over avocados (about 1 tablespoon); gently stir in salsa.
3. Serve over shredded lettuce.
