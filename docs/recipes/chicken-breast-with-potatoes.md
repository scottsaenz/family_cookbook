# Chicken Breast with Potatoes

**Cook:** 1 hr

## Ingredients

- Chicken breast
- Potato sticks
- Italian seasoning

## Instructions

1. Lay chicken breast on pan.
2. Cover with potato sticks.
3. Sprinkle Italian seasoning.
4. Cover and bake at 350°F for 1 hour.
5. Potatoes should be brown and chicken cooked in middle.
