# Cabbage, Noodles, Bacon, and Onion

## Ingredients

- bacon
- onion
- cabbage
- noodles

## Instructions

1. Cook bacon, break into pieces.
2. Cook onions in bacon grease.
3. Add cabbage and cook until tender.
4. When cabbage is tender, add cooked noodles and bacon.
