# Macaroni and Cheese

**Cook:** 20-30 min

## Ingredients

- Macaroni
- 1 stick butter
- Shredded cheese
- Pepper
- Mustard
- Milk
- Paprika

## Instructions

1. Cook and drain macaroni.
2. Put macaroni in a dish.
3. Add 1 stick butter and shredded cheese.
4. Add pepper and mustard.
5. Pour over milk.
6. Sprinkle paprika on top.
7. Bake at 375°F for 20-30 minutes.
