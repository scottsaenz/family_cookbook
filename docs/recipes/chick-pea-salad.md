# Chick Pea Salad

**Servings:** 6

## Ingredients

- 1 15.5 oz. can Goya Chick Peas, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 cup red pepper, chopped
- 1 small red or sweet onion, chopped
- 1 medium cucumber, chopped
- 1 Tbsp. balsamic vinegar
- 3 Tbsp. Goya Olive Oil
- 1/4 tsp. each garlic powder and oregano
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Toss all ingredients together, chill and serve.
