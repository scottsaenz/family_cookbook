# City Chicken

**Cook:** 45 min

## Ingredients

- Pork
- Egg
- Flour

## Instructions

1. Cut pork into cubes. Put on skewers.
2. Shake in seasoned flour. Dip in egg.
3. Shake in flour again.
4. Fry slightly.
5. Bake in oven at 350°F for 45 min.
6. Check for doneness. May need to be baked longer.
