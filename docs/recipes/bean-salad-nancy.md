# Bean Salad Nancy

## Ingredients

- 1 can green beans
- 1 can yellow beans
- 1 can kidney beans - drain & rinse
- 1 can chickpeas or navy beans
- Sliced onion
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 2/3 cup vinegar
- 3/4 cup white vinegar + 1/4 cup cider vinegar
- 1/3 cup salad oil
- Salt & pepper to taste
