# Turkey Tart

**Prep:** 15 min | **Total:** 45 min | **Servings:** 6

## Ingredients

- 1 cup diced cooked turkey
- 1 1/2 cups cooked broccoli
- 1 9-inch deep-dish pie crust
- 3 eggs
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp pepper
- 1 1/4 cups half-and-half
- 1 cup grated Parmesan
- 1 Tbsp parsley

## Instructions

1. 1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Sprinkle turkey and broccoli over crust.
2. 2. In a bowl, whisk together eggs, salt, and pepper. Stir in half-and-half, Parmesan, and parsley. Pour mixture over turkey and broccoli.
3. 3. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes or until filling is set and tart is golden brown.
