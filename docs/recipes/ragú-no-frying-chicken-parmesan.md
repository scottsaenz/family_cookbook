# Ragú No Frying Chicken Parmesan

**Prep:** 10 minutes | **Cook:** 30 minutes | **Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

- 4 boneless chicken breast halves
- 1 egg, beaten
- 3/4 cup Italian-seasoned dry breadcrumbs
- 1 jar (1 pound 10 ounces) Ragú Old World Style Pasta Sauce
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese

## Instructions

1. Dip chicken in egg, then in breadcrumbs.
2. Arrange chicken in 13-by-9-inch baking dish. Bake uncovered at 400°F for 20 minutes.
3. Pour Pasta Sauce over chicken; top with cheese. Bake 10 minutes or until chicken is thoroughly cooked.
