# Pink Arctic Freeze

## Ingredients

- 1 (8-oz.) soft cream cheese
- 2 T. mayonnaise
- 2 T. sugar
- 1 (11-oz.) can white fruit cocktail
- 1 (20-oz.) can crushed pineapple (undrained)
- 1/2 c. chopped walnuts
- 2 c. Cool Whip

## Instructions

1. Combine cream cheese, mayonnaise, and sugar.
2. Add fruit and walnuts.
3. Fold in Cool Whip.
4. Pour into 9-inch pan and freeze 3 hours or overnight.
5. Cut into squares and serve.
6. Take from freezer about 15 minutes before cutting.
