# Homestyle Green Beans

**Servings:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- 2 (14.5-oz) cans Italian green beans (drain 1 can)
- 1 tablespoon cooked bacon pieces
- 1/4 teaspoon seasoned salt
- 1/8 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 drops liquid smoke flavoring

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients in microwave-safe bowl (including liquid from 1 can of beans).
2. Cover and microwave on HIGH 5-6 minutes, stirring once, or until thoroughly heated.
