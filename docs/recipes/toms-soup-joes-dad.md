# Tom's Soup - Joe's Dad

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 lb smoked sausage
- 1 lb bacon
- 2 cans diced potatoes
- 1 can corn
- 1 can peas
- 1 can green beans
- rice or sliced potatoes
- onions

## Instructions

1. Brown beef, bacon, and sausage.
2. Add 2 cans diced potatoes, 1 can corn, 1 can peas, and 1 can green beans.
3. Add rice or sliced potatoes as desired.
4. If too sweet, add water.
