# Angel Food Cake with Crushed Pineapple

**Cook:** 25 mins

## Ingredients

- 1 Angel Food Cake
- 1 can Crushed Pineapple

## Instructions

1. Mix Angel Food Cake with Crushed Pineapple.
2. Pour into a 9x13 baking pan.
3. Bake at 350°F for 25 mins or until golden brown.
