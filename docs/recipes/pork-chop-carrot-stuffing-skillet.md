# Pork Chop & Carrot Stuffing Skillet

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 20 min | **Servings:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- 6 boneless pork chops, 1/2 inch thick (1 1/2 lb.)
- 1 Tbsp. oil
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 3 cups baby carrots, cut in half lengthwise
- 1 pkg. (6 oz.) STOVE TOP Stuffing Mix for Pork
- 1 cup KRAFT Shredded Low-Moisture Part-Skim Mozzarella Cheese

## Instructions

1. BROWN chops in hot oil in large skillet on high heat. Reduce heat to medium-high; cook 10 min. on each side or until done (160°F), adding onions after the first 5 min. Remove chops from skillet; cover chops to keep warm.
2. ADD carrots and 2 cups water to skillet; stir. Bring to boil.
3. RETURN chops to skillet; sprinkle with cheese. Reduce heat to low; cover. Cook 5 min. or until cheese is melted.
