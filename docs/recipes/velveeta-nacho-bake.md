# VELVEETA Nacho Bake

**Prep:** 15 min | **Cook:** 20 min | **Servings:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 pkg. (12 oz.) VELVEETA Shells & Cheese Dinner
- 1 lb. ground beef
- 1 pkg. (1 oz.) TACO BELL HOME ORIGINALS Taco Seasoning Mix
- 3/4 cup BREAKSTONE'S or KNUDSEN Sour Cream
- 3/4 cup KRAFT Shredded Cheddar Cheese
- 1 cup TACO BELL HOME ORIGINALS Thick 'N Chunky Salsa
- 1 cup coarsely crushed tortilla chips

## Instructions

1. PREHEAT oven to 400°F. Prepare Dinner following Basic Directions on package.
2. Brown meat; add taco seasoning mix and water. Simmer 5 minutes.
3. Stir sour cream into prepared Dinner. Spoon half into 8-inch square baking dish; top with layers of meat mixture, 1/2 cup of the cheese and the remaining Dinner mixture.
4. Cover with foil. Bake 20 minutes. Top with salsa and chips. Bake, uncovered, an additional 5 minutes.
