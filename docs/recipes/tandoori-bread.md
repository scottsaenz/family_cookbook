# Tandoori Bread

## Ingredients

- Marinate in Tandoori for 2 days

## Instructions

1. Grill on the barbecue for a few minutes.
2. After about 2 minutes, season with kosher salt and pepper before grilling on both sides.
3. Wrap in foil for about 5 minutes.
