# Easy Breakfast Strata

**Cook:** 60-70 minutes

## Ingredients

- 8 slices bread, cubed
- 10 slices bacon, cooked
- 3 cups (12 oz) shredded cheese
- 2 cups sliced fresh mushrooms
- 1 (10 oz) pkg frozen asparagus
- 2 tbsp margarine
- 2 tbsp flour
- 1-2 tbsp dry mustard
- 2 tsp basil
- 1 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. In a large skillet, brown sausage; drain.
2. Beat eggs in a large bowl; add remaining ingredients.
3. Mix well; pour into greased 9x13-inch baking pan.
4. Cover and refrigerate for 8 hours or overnight.
5. Bake at 350°F for 60-70 minutes or until knife inserted comes out clean.
