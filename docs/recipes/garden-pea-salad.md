# Garden Pea Salad

## Ingredients

- 1-15 oz can La Seur Peas
- 1/4 cup chopped onion
- 2 boiled eggs, crumbled
- Salt & Pepper
- 2-3 tablespoons mayo
- Chopped pimento

## Instructions

1. Drain peas and add everything.
