# Boneless Pork Chops

**Cook:** 6 hours on low or 4 hours on high

## Ingredients

- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 1 pkg dry ranch dressing
- boneless pork chops

## Instructions

1. In crockpot, layer pork chops.
2. Add soup and sprinkle dry ranch dressing.
3. Cook on low for 6 hours or high for 4 hours.
