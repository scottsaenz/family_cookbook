# Watergate Salad

**Prep:** 5 min | **Servings:** 8 servings, about 1/2 cup each

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Jet-Puffed Miniature Marshmallows
- 1 pkg. (3.4 oz.) Jell-O Pistachio Flavor Instant Pudding
- 1 can (20 oz.) Dole Crushed Pineapple, in juice, undrained
- 1/2 cup chopped Planters Pecans
- 1 1/2 cups thawed Cool Whip Whipped Topping

## Instructions

1. Combine marshmallows, dry pudding mix, pineapple and nuts in large bowl.
2. Stir in Cool Whip. Refrigerate 1 hour.

## Notes

HOW TO THAW COOL WHIP WHIPPED TOPPING: Place unopened tub in refrigerator. An 8-oz. tub will be completely thawed in 4 hours. Do not thaw in microwave.
