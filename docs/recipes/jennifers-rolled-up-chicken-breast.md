# Jennifer's Rolled Up Chicken Breast

## Ingredients

- Chicken breast (thin sliced)
- Provolone cheese or mozzarella cheese
- Spinach (chopped)
- Bacon slices

## Instructions

1. Put spinach and cheese on chicken breast.
2. Roll up and wrap bacon around the chicken.
3. Bake until done.
