# Meatballs

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp pepper
- 1/2 cup parsley
- 1 cup breadcrumbs
- 1/4 cup parmesan cheese
- 1/2 cup milk
- 2 eggs
- 1 lb hot Italian sausage
