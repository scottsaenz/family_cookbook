# Hot Fudge Pie

**Cook:** 30-35 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 stick butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 3 TBS cocoa (Dutch)
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Combine butter, sugar, eggs, cocoa, flour, and salt. Mix until smooth.
3. Pour mixture in pie plate.
4. Bake for 30-35 minutes until center is firm.
5. Serve with whipped cream or ice cream.
