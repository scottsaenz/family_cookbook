# 14 Time Scalloped Potatoes

**Cook:** 1 hr 10 min

## Ingredients

- 4 potatoes
- 2 cans mushroom soup - milk
- onion
- salt & pepper
- garlic
- shredded cheese
- bacon pieces

## Instructions

1. Heat mushroom soup with 1 can milk, onion, salt, pepper & garlic.
2. Pour sauce in casserole dish, layer potatoes & sauce.
3. Cook until done - about 1 hr.
4. Add cheese & bacon on top, cook 10 min more.
