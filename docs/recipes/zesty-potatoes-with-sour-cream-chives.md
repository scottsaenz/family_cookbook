# Zesty Potatoes with Sour Cream & Chives

## Ingredients

- 4 medium potatoes, cut into 1 1/2 inch pieces
- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon chopped chives
- Sour cream, if desired

## Instructions

1. Seal Ziploc® Zip 'n Steam™ bag and shake to mix ingredients. Place bag in microwave.
2. Cook on full power for 7 minutes or until tender.
3. Allow bag to stand 1 minute before handling. Gently shake bag to distribute seasonings. Carefully open bag and remove contents.
4. Sprinkle with 1 tablespoon chopped chives. Add salt and pepper to taste. Serve with sour cream, if desired.
