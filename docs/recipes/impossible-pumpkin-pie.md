# Impossible Pumpkin Pie

**Cook:** 50-55 minutes

## Ingredients

- 3/4 c. sugar
- 1/2 c. Bisquick
- 2 T. margarine
- 1 can evaporated milk
- 2 eggs
- 1 can (15 oz) pumpkin
- 2 1/2 t. pumpkin pie spice
- 2 t. vanilla

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 325 degrees.
2. Beat all ingredients until smooth (using a blender if desired).
3. Pour into a greased 9 inch pie plate.
4. Bake 50-55 minutes.
