# Ranch Coleslaw with Bacon

**Servings:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 pkg. (16 oz.) coleslaw blend (cabbage slaw mix)
- 4 slices cooked and crumbled Oscar Mayer Bacon
- 1/2 cup Kraft Ranch Dressing

## Instructions

1. Combine 1 pkg. (16 oz.) coleslaw blend (cabbage slaw mix) and 4 slices cooked and crumbled Oscar Mayer Bacon in large bowl.
2. Add 1/2 cup Kraft Ranch Dressing; toss to coat.
