# Shrimp (Fresh) with Lemon & Italian Dressing

**Cook:** 15 mins

## Ingredients

- 1 stick of butter
- 1 lemon
- Fresh shrimp
- Dried Italian dressing

## Instructions

1. Melt stick of butter in pan.
2. Slice one lemon and layer on top of butter.
3. Put down fresh shrimp.
4. Sprinkle dried Italian dressing on top.
5. Bake at 350°F for 15 mins.
