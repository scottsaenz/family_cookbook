# Shredded Chicken Alfredo Lasagna

## Ingredients

- 9 lasagna noodles
- 2 1/2 cups alfredo sauce
- 3 cups cooked, shredded chicken
- oregano
- garlic salt
- 3 cups mozzarella cheese

## Instructions

1. Spray 8x8 pan.
2. Boil noodles.
3. Layer noodles, sauce, chicken, oregano, garlic salt, and mozzarella cheese.
4. Repeat layers.
5. Pour 3/4 alfredo sauce over top.
6. Spread sauce over.
