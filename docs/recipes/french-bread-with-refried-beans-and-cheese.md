# French Bread with Refried Beans and Cheese

## Ingredients

- French bread
- Refried beans
- Cheese
- Chopped tomatoes
- White onion
- Lime juice
- Chopped cilantro

## Instructions

1. Spread refried beans and cheese on French bread.
2. Bake until warm and melted.
3. Top with chopped tomatoes, white onion, lime juice, and chopped cilantro.
