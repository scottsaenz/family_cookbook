# Honey Walnut Plums

**Servings:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 4 medium plums, cut into 8 wedges each
- 1/2 cup walnuts
- 3 tablespoons honey
- 1 1/2 teaspoons cornstarch
- Pinch of salt
- Pinch of cloves
- Pinch of cinnamon

## Instructions

1. Combine in Medium Ziploc® Zip'n Steam® bag: plums, walnuts, honey, cornstarch, salt, cloves, and cinnamon.
2. Seal bag and shake to mix ingredients.
3. Cook on full power for 2 minutes or until plums are slightly soft. Microwave for additional 30-second intervals until done.
4. Allow bag to stand for 1 minute before handling. Shake bag gently to distribute sauce. Carefully open bag.
5. Serve with yogurt or ice cream, if desired.
