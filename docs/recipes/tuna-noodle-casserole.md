# Tuna Noodle Casserole

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 35 min | **Servings:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- 2 cans (10 3/4 oz. each) Campbell's Cream of Mushroom Soup (Regular or 25% Less Sodium)
- 1 cup milk
- 2 cups frozen peas
- 2 cans (about 12 oz. each) tuna, drained
- 4 cups hot, cooked medium egg noodles
- 2 tbsp. dry bread crumbs
- 1 tbsp. butter, melted

## Instructions

1. Stir soup, milk, peas, tuna and noodles in a 3-qt. casserole.
2. Bake at 400°F. for 30 min. or until hot. Stir.
3. Mix bread crumbs with butter and sprinkle over tuna mixture. Bake for 5 min. more.

## Notes

Cheesy Tuna Melt: In place of bread crumbs, sprinkle 1/2 cup shredded Cheddar over hot casserole. Chicken Noodle Casserole: Substitute about 2 cups cooked cubed chicken or Swanson premium chunk chicken, drained, for tuna.
