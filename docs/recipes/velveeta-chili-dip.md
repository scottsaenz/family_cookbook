# Velveeta Chili Dip

## Ingredients

- 1-16 oz pack Velveeta cheese, cut up
- 1-15 oz can chili

## Instructions

1. Mix Velveeta and chili in 2 qt microwaveable bowl.
2. Microwave for 5 min or until cheese is melted.
