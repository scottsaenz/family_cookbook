# Ham Casserole

**Cook:** 70 minutes

## Ingredients

- 14 oz torn bread
- 8 eggs
- Evaporated milk
- Ham
- Broccoli
- Green pepper
- Onion
- Cheddar cheese

## Instructions

1. Spray pan with oil.
2. Put in ham, broccoli, green pepper, and onion.
3. Add egg mixture.
4. Put cheddar cheese on top.
5. Cover and bake at 350°F for 70 minutes.
