# Fresh Cheese Recipe

## Ingredients

- white rice
- green peppers
- onions
- small can tomato sauce
- chicken
- beef
- pork
- fresh cheese, shredded

## Instructions

1. Saute onions and green peppers.
2. Cook rice.
3. Cook chicken, beef, and pork.
4. Put rice on plate.
5. Put onions, peppers, and meat on top.
6. Top with shredded fresh cheese.
