# Caramel Apple Cobbler

**Cook:** 45 mins

## Ingredients

- 1 box yellow cake mix
- 2 cans apple pie filling
- 1 jar caramel topping
- 1 stick butter

## Instructions

1. Butter a 9x13 pan.
2. Pour apple pie filling in the pan.
3. Drizzle caramel topping over the filling.
4. Sprinkle dry cake mix evenly over the top.
5. Pour melted butter over the cake mix.
6. Bake at 350°F for 45 mins.
