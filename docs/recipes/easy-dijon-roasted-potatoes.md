# Easy Dijon Roasted Potatoes

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 30 min | **Servings:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lbs. small red potatoes, cut in half
- 1/3 cup olive oil
- 1/4 cup FRENCH'S Dijon Mustard
- 2 tsp. dried rosemary, thyme or oregano leaves
- to taste salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. PLACE potatoes in large baking dish.
2. MIX remaining ingredients. Drizzle over potatoes. Toss to coat.
3. BAKE at 425°F for 30 to 40 min. stirring often until potatoes are tender.
