# Classic Chocolate Fudge

**Prep:** 10 min. plus refrigerating | **Servings:** 24 servings

## Ingredients

- 2 pkg. (8 squares each) BAKER'S Semi-Sweet Chocolate
- 1 can (14 oz.) EAGLE BRAND sweetened condensed milk
- 1 cup chopped PLANTERS Walnuts
- 2 tsp. vanilla

## Instructions

1. LINE 8-inch square pan with foil.
2. Microwave chocolate and milk in microwaveable bowl on HIGH 2 to 3 min. or until chocolate is almost melted, stirring after 2 min.
3. Stir until completely melted.
4. Stir in nuts and vanilla.
5. SPREAD into pan.
6. REFRIGERATE 2 hours or until firm.
7. Use foil to lift fudge from pan; cut into pieces.
8. Store in refrigerator up to 1 week.
9. For creamier fudge, let stand at room temperature 1 hour before serving.
