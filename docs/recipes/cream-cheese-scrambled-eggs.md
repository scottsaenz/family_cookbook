# Cream Cheese Scrambled Eggs

## Ingredients

- cream cheese
- 6 eggs
- season salt
- chives
- 2 slices of cheese

## Instructions

1. Melt cream cheese in bowl.
2. Add 6 eggs to cream cheese, beat with whisk.
3. Season with salt.
4. Add chives.
5. Microwave, stirring often.
6. Add 2 slices of cheese.
