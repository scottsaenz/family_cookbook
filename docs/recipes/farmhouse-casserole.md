# Farmhouse Casserole

**Cook:** 45 min

## Ingredients

- Potatoes
- Eggs
- Milk
- Cheese
- Ham
- Green onions

## Instructions

1. Place potatoes on bottom of casserole dish.
2. Sprinkle cheese, ham, and green onions.
3. Add egg mixture.
4. Bake uncovered for 45 minutes.
