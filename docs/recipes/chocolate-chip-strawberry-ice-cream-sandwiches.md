# Chocolate Chip Strawberry Ice Cream Sandwiches

**Prep:** 15 min | **Total:** 2 hr | **Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

- 8 Pepperidge Farm Soft Baked Montauk milk chocolate cookies
- 1/2 (14-oz) container Häagen-Dazs strawberry ice cream, slightly softened
- 8 strawberries, sliced [optional]
- 1/2 (11-oz) package semi-sweet chocolate chips
- 1 Tbsp canola oil

## Instructions

1. Arrange 4 cookies flat side up in a single layer. Spread with ice cream. Top with berries, if desired, and another cookie. Wrap in plastic and freeze for at least 1 hour.
2. Combine chips and oil in a small bowl. Microwave on medium in 30-second increments until chocolate is melted. Let cool slightly, then dip half of ice cream sandwich into chocolate. Wrap cooled sandwich in plastic and freeze for 1 hour.
