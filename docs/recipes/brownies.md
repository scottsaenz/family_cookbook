# Brownies

**Cook:** 20-25 min

## Ingredients

- 1 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1/3 cup cocoa
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp baking powder
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Mix all ingredients together.
3. Pour into an 8x8 or 9x9 square pan.
4. Bake for 20-25 minutes.

## Notes

Can add nuts if desired.
