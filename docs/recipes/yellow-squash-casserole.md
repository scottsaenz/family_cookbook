# Yellow Squash Casserole

**Cook:** 30-45 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 TBS butter
- 4 cups sliced yellow squash
- 1 medium chopped onion
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup grated cheddar cheese
- 1 cup milk
- 2 TBS butter
- Salt, pepper
- Ritz crackers

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Melt 2 TBS butter in a skillet and sauté squash and onions until tender.
3. Whisk eggs in a large bowl lightly, add cheese and milk.
4. Add squash, stir to combine, add more melted butter, salt, and pepper.
5. Spray a casserole dish and pour mixture in, top with Ritz crackers.
6. Bake for 30-45 minutes.
