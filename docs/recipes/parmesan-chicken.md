# Parmesan Chicken

## Ingredients

- Chicken thighs
- Flour
- Seasoning (such as Lawry's)
- 2 eggs
- Parmesan cheese
- Bread crumbs
- Melted butter

## Instructions

1. Coat chicken with seasoned flour.
2. Dip in slightly beaten eggs.
3. Press in cracker crumbs.
4. Mix 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese, 1/4 cup bread crumbs with 1 tbsp melted butter.
5. Top chicken with the mixture.
6. Bake at 350°F.
