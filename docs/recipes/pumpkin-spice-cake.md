# Pumpkin & Spice Cake

**Cook:** 15-17 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 15 oz can pumpkin
- 1 spice cake mix

## Instructions

1. Mix together pumpkin and cake mix.
2. Put into greased baking sheet.
3. Bake at 350°F for 15-17 minutes.
