# Strawberry Punch Bowl Cake

## Ingredients

- 1 angel food cake (large)
- 1 large box instant vanilla pudding mix
- 1 cup milk
- 1 tub cool whip
- 1 qt fresh strawberries, sliced

## Instructions

1. Tear cake into small pieces.
2. Prepare pudding as directed, add milk.
3. Fold cool whip into pudding mixture and save a cup for topping.
4. Place 1/3 cake pieces into bowl, top with 1/3 pudding mix and 1/3 strawberries.
5. Repeat and top with remaining pudding mixture.
6. Top with remaining cool whip, garnish with strawberries.
7. Refrigerate.
