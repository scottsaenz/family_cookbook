# Sweet Potato and Hamburger Casserole

**Cook:** 1-2 hours

## Ingredients

- Sliced potatoes
- Green beans
- Crumbled hamburger
- Cheese

## Instructions

1. In casserole dish, add sliced potatoes, green beans, and crumbled hamburger.
2. Place all more sliced potatoes over mixture.
3. Bake at 350°F for 1-2 hours.
4. Cover with aluminum foil.
5. Make topping of cheese and place over top.
