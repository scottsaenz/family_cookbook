# Biscuits and Gravy

## Ingredients

- 1 cup cheese
- 2 cups milk
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 4 tablespoons flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 egg

## Instructions

1. Cut biscuits into gravy.
2. Beat 6 eggs.
3. Add 1/2 cup milk to 2 tablespoons butter and 4 tablespoons flour.
4. Add 2 cups milk with cheese.
5. Bake at 350 degrees for 35-40 minutes.
