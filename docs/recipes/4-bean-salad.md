# 4 Bean Salad

## Ingredients

- 1 can green beans
- 1 can wax beans
- 1 can small pea kidney beans
- 1 can chickpeas
- 1/2 cup vinegar (white + cider)
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1/3 cup oil
- Salt
- Pepper
