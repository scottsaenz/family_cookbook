# Amanda's Fruit Salad

## Ingredients

- 2 cans 15 oz fruit cocktail
- 16 ounces sour cream
- 1 small bag marshmallows
- maraschino cherries
- pineapple

## Instructions

1. Drain fruit cocktail.
2. Add marshmallows, sour cream, and cherries to fruit cocktail.
3. Chill overnight.
