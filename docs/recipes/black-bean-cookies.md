# Black Bean Cookies

**Cook:** 10-12 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 can black beans - low sodium
- 2 tbsp milk
- 2 tbsp peanut butter
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 tbsp flour
- 1 tbsp cocoa powder
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tsp vanilla
- Pinch of cinnamon
- 1/3 cup dark chocolate
- 1/3 cup chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. Rinse black beans well.
3. Combine black beans, milk, peanut butter, and olive oil in a blender until smooth.
4. Add flour, cocoa powder, sugar, baking powder, vanilla, and cinnamon to the mixture.
5. Blend until mousse-like.
6. Stir in 1/3 cup chocolate chips.
7. Scoop onto baking sheet.
8. Sprinkle chips on top.
9. Bake for 10-12 minutes.
