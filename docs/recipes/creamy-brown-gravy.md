# Creamy Brown Gravy

**Cook:** 3 min | **Servings:** 4 servings, 4 meatballs and 1/3 cup sauce each

## Ingredients

- 1 jar (12 oz.) beef gravy
- 1/2 cup Breakstone's or Knudsen Sour Cream
- 2 Tbsp. chopped fresh parsley
- 16 hot meatballs

## Instructions

1. Mix 1 jar (12 oz.) beef gravy, 1/2 cup Breakstone's or Knudsen Sour Cream and 2 Tbsp. chopped fresh parsley in large skillet.
2. Cook on medium-low heat 3 min. or until heated through, stirring occasionally.
3. Add 16 hot meatballs; stir to evenly coat.
