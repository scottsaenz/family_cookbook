# Kentucky Seven Layer Salad

## Ingredients

- 1 head of lettuce torn
- 1 bunch of green onions chopped
- 2 (10 oz) boxes of frozen peas thawed, drained not cooked
- Layer of chopped ham
- Layer of eggs
- 1 1/2 cups mayo + 1 1/2 cups sour cream
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup grated cheese

## Instructions

1. Place lettuce pieces in bottom of a large casserole dish.
2. Sprinkle green onions over the lettuce and then the peas over the onions.
3. Sprinkle the ham over the peas.
4. Then place a layer of sliced hard boiled eggs.
5. Make a dressing by combining the sour cream, sugar, and mayo.
6. Pour the dressing over the eggs.
7. Sprinkle the cheese over the dressing.
8. Chill before serving.
