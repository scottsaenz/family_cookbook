# Tuna or Chicken Chickpea Salad

## Ingredients

- 3 oz tuna or chicken (poached in water)
- 1/4 cup garbanzo beans
- 1 tbsp mayo
- 1 tbsp brown [unclear]
- 1/2 medium apple, diced
- 2 tbsp shredded carrot
- 1 celery stalk, diced
- raisins
- peanuts

## Instructions

1. Combine 3 oz tuna or chicken (poached in water) with 1/4 cup garbanzo beans.
2. Add 1 tbsp mayo and 1 tbsp brown [unclear].
3. Mix in 1/2 medium apple, diced, 2 tbsp shredded carrot, and 1 celery stalk, diced.
4. Add raisins and peanuts to taste.
