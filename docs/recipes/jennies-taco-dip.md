# Jennie's Taco Dip

## Ingredients

- 1 can refried beans
- 8 oz cream cheese
- 8 oz sour cream
- 1 pack taco seasoning
- Shredded cheese
- Shredded lettuce
- Sliced black olives

## Instructions

1. 1st layer: Spread 1 can refried beans on the bottom of a dish.
2. 2nd layer: Mix 8 oz cream cheese and 8 oz sour cream with 1 pack taco seasoning. Spread over the beans.
3. 3rd layer: Add shredded cheese.
4. 4th layer: Add shredded lettuce.
5. 5th layer: Top with more shredded cheese and sliced black olives.
