# Armenian Lasagna

## Ingredients

- 3 lbs ground beef
- 4-6 garlic cloves
- 2 large cans diced tomatoes
- 2-15 oz cans tomato sauce
- 1-8 oz can tomato paste
- 1 tablespoon oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
- Salt and pepper
- 1 lb lasagna noodles
- 1 lb mozzarella cheese
- 2 lbs ricotta cheese
- 1/2 cup parmesan cheese
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley

## Instructions

1. Brown 3 lbs ground beef with 4-6 garlic cloves.
2. Add 2 large cans diced tomatoes, 2-15 oz cans tomato sauce, 1-8 oz can tomato paste.
3. Add 1 tablespoon oregano, 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon, salt and pepper.
4. Simmer for 2 hours.
5. Cook 1 lb lasagna noodles.
6. Slice 1 lb mozzarella cheese.
7. Layer noodles, sauce, ricotta cheese, mozzarella cheese, and parmesan cheese.
8. Top with fresh parsley.
