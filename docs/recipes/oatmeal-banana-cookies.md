# Oatmeal + Banana Cookies

**Cook:** 15 minutes | **Servings:** 48

## Ingredients

- 2 bananas
- 1 cup oatmeal
- raisins
- chocolate
- 1 packet of sugar

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 315°F.
2. Mix all ingredients together.
3. Bake for 15 minutes.
