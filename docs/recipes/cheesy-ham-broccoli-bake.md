# Cheesy Ham & Broccoli Bake

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 30 min | **Servings:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 pkg. (6 oz.) STOVE TOP Stuffing Mix for Pork
- 1-1/2 cups hot water
- 3 cups cubed ham
- 1 pkg. (10 oz.) frozen broccoli florets, thawed, drained
- 1 can (10-3/4 oz.) condensed cream of mushroom soup
- 1/4 cup milk
- 1-1/2 cups KRAFT Shredded Cheddar Cheese

## Instructions

1. HEAT oven to 375°F.
2. ADD 1-1/2 cups hot water to stuffing mix; stir just until moistened. Set aside.
3. MIX ham and broccoli in 13x9-inch baking dish. Combine soup, milk and cheese; pour over ham mixture. Top with prepared stuffing.
4. BAKE 30 min. or until heated through.
