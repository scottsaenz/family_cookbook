# Bean Burrito Supreme

**Cook:** 25-30 minutes | **Servings:** 12 large burritos

## Ingredients

- 2 cans refried beans
- 1 pkg. (12) flour tortillas
- 1 lb. your favorite cheese
- 4 fresh tomatoes
- 2 bunches green onions
- 1 can black olives (optional)
- 1 oz. jar salsa
- 1 sm. container sour cream

## Instructions

1. Warm beans separately.
2. Preheat oven to 375 degrees.
3. Spoon beans into tortilla, add grated cheese, diced tomatoes and chopped onions.
4. If you desire, add the black olives.
5. Roll tortilla lengthwise and place in oblong pan.
6. Sprinkle additional cheese on top, along with salsa.
7. Bake at 375 degrees approximately 25 to 30 minutes.
8. Garnish with more salsa and sour cream.

## Notes

Very colorful!
