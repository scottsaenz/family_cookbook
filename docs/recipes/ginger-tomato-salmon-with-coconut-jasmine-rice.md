# Ginger-Tomato Salmon with Coconut Jasmine Rice

**Total:** 30 minutes | **Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lb salmon fillets, skin removed
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 teaspoons ginger spice paste
- 1/2 cup presliced green onions
- 1 (14.5-oz) can petite diced tomatoes
- 2 1/4 cups chicken stock
- 1 (13.6-oz) can coconut milk
- 1 cup jasmine rice
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt

## Notes

Complete your meal with a fresh salad blend, water rolls, and Key lime pie for dessert. You can substitute any firm white fish for the salmon.
