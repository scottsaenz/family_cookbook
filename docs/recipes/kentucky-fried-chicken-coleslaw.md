# Kentucky Fried Chicken Coleslaw

**Servings:** 8

## Ingredients

- 8 cups finely chopped cabbage (1 head) or the equivalent of bagged slaw mixture
- 1/4 cup shredded carrot, if not using bagged slaw mixture
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/8 teaspoon pepper
- 1/4 cup milk
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1/4 cup buttermilk
- 1 1/2 teaspoons white vinegar
- 2 1/2 tablespoons lemon juice

## Instructions

1. Be sure the cabbage and carrots are chopped into very fine pieces, about the size of rice kernels.
2. In a large bowl, combine the sugar, salt, pepper, milk, mayonnaise, buttermilk, vinegar and lemon juice; beat until smooth.
3. Add the cabbage and carrots. Mix well.
4. Cover and refrigerate at least 2 hours before serving.
