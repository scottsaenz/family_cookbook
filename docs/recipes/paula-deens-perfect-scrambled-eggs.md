# Paula Deen's Perfect Scrambled Eggs

**Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

- 8 large eggs
- 2 heaping TBS sour cream
- Kosher salt
- Ground pepper
- 1/2 - 3/4 cup cheddar cheese
- 2 TBS unsalted butter

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, beat the eggs, sour cream, 1 TBS water, salt, and pepper to taste.
2. Melt butter in pan and cook, stirring occasionally.
3. Stir in cheese and serve with biscuits.
