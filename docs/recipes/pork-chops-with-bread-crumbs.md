# Pork Chops with Bread Crumbs

## Ingredients

- Pork chops
- Seasoned flour
- Seasoning salt
- Melted butter
- Corn flake crumbs

## Instructions

1. Put pork chops in seasoned flour.
2. Dip in melted butter and corn flake crumbs.
3. Put on greased baking sheet.

## Notes

Better than with just corn flake crumbs.
