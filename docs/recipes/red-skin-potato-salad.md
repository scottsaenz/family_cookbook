# Red Skin Potato Salad

**Cook:** 12 min

## Ingredients

- red skin potatoes
- 3 medium potatoes
- 3 eggs
- mayo
- 1 tbsp vinegar

## Instructions

1. Cube red skin potatoes.
2. Boil about 12 minutes.
3. Rinse in cold water.
4. Hard boil 3 eggs and peel.
5. Mix mayo with 1 tbsp vinegar.
