# Ultimate 7-Layer Dip

**Prep:** 10 min (plus refrigeration) | **Servings:** 48 servings, about 2 Tbsp. each

## Ingredients

- 1 can (16 oz.) refried beans
- 1 Tbsp. Taco Bell Home Originals Taco Seasoning Mix
- 1 cup Breakstone's Sour Cream
- 1 cup Taco Bell Home Originals Salsa
- 1 cup shredded lettuce
- 1 cup Kraft Mexican Style Shredded Cheese
- 1/2 cup chopped green onions
- 2 Tbsp. sliced pitted ripe olives

## Instructions

1. MIX beans and taco seasoning mix. Spread onto bottom of 9-inch pie plate.
2. LAYER remaining ingredients over bean mixture; cover. Refrigerate several hours or until chilled.
3. SERVE with tortilla chips or assorted crackers.
