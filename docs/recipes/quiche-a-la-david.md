# Quiche a la David

**Cook:** 1 hour

## Ingredients

- 1 TJ's refrigerated pie crust
- 1 bag of TJ's fresh spinach
- 1 package of bacon (cooked and broken into pieces)
- 1 cup shredded jack cheddar cheese blend
- 1 cup milk
- 5 eggs
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Place pie crust in a pie plate.
2. Tear up spinach and put on crust.
3. Sprinkle bacon, then cheese on top.
4. Beat eggs with milk, salt and pepper and slowly pour over the fillings so it can seep in.
5. Bake at 350°F for 1 hour or until golden brown and set.
6. Enjoy!
