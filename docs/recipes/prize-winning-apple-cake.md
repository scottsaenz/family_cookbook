# Prize Winning Apple Cake

**Cook:** 1 hour

## Ingredients

- 2 c. sugar
- 1 c. light vegetable oil
- 2 eggs
- 3 c. flour
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 1 tsp. salt
- 2 tsp. cinnamon
- 3 c. apples, peeled and chopped
- 1 c. nuts, chopped
- 1 c. raisins (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°.
2. Believe it or not, you put all ingredients into one large bowl and mix with your hands as you would a meatloaf gently but thoroughly. Do not use a spoon or mixer.
3. Place in ungreased 9 x 13 pan.
4. Bake for 1 hour.
5. Cool before cutting. Keeps well and is moist.
