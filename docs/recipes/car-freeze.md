# Car Freeze

## Ingredients

- 1 graham cracker crust
- 2 pkgs unsweetened Kool-Aid (orange or watermelon)
- 1 8 oz softened cream cheese
- 1 14 oz condensed milk
- 1 8 oz Cool Whip

## Instructions

1. In large bowl, combine cream cheese and Kool-Aid.
2. Add milk and mix till smooth.
3. Fold in Cool Whip, stir until mixed well.
4. Spoon into crust.
5. Refrigerate for 1-2 hours.
