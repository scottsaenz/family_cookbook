# Hawaiian Crock Pot Chicken

**Cook:** 8 hours

## Ingredients

- 2 lb chicken (thighs)
- 1 cup pineapple juice
- 1/2 cup brown sugar
- 2/3 cup soy sauce

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients.
2. Cook on low in crock pot for 8 hours.
3. Serve with brown rice.
