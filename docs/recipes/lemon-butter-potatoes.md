# Lemon-Butter Potatoes

## Ingredients

- Cooking spray
- 1 (24-oz) bag Dutch Yellow Baby Potatoes
- 2 tablespoons water
- 4 tablespoons Kerrygold Irish Unsalted Butter
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 teaspoon herb-seasoning salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 450°F. Coat baking sheet with cooking spray.
2. Cut potatoes in half; place in microwave-safe bowl with water.
3. Cover potatoes and microwave on HIGH 7–8 minutes or until tender. Cut butter into small pieces; set aside.
4. Squeeze juice of lemon over potatoes; stir in butter and seasoning. Spread potatoes in single layer on baking sheet. Bake 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, or until golden. Serve.
