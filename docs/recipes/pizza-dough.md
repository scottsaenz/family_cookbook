# Pizza Dough

## Ingredients

- 1 cup warm water
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 pkg dry active yeast
- 2 1/2 cups bread flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp olive oil

## Instructions

1. Mix water, sugar, and yeast. Let sit for 10 minutes.
2. Add flour, salt, and olive oil.
3. Mix together.
