# Green Bean Casserole

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 30 min | **Servings:** 12 servings

## Ingredients

- 2 cans (10 3/4 oz. each) Campbell's Cream of Mushroom Soup (Regular, 98% Fat Free or 25% Less Sodium)
- 1 cup milk
- 2 tsp. soy sauce
- 1/4 tsp. ground black pepper
- 8 cups cooked cut green beans
- 1 can (6 oz.) French's French Fried Onions (2 2/3 cups)

## Instructions

1. Stir soup, milk, soy sauce, black pepper, beans and 1 1/3 cups onions in a 3-qt. casserole.
2. Bake at 350°F for 25 min. or until hot. Stir.
3. Top with remaining onions. Bake for 5 min. more.

## Notes

Go Nuts! Toast 1/2 cup sliced almonds. Add with remaining onions. Bring on the Bacon: Cook 4 slices bacon until crisp and crumble. Add to green bean mixture.
