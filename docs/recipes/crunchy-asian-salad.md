# Crunchy Asian Salad

**Prep:** 15 minutes | **Cook:** 5 minutes | **Total:** 20 minutes | **Servings:** 8 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 envelope GOOD SEASONS Italian Salad Dressing & Recipe Mix
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 2 Tbsp. soy sauce
- 2 pkg. (3 oz. each) ramen noodle soup mix
- 2 pkg. (16 oz. each) coleslaw blend
- 4 green onions, sliced
- 1/2 cup PLANTERS Dry Roasted Sunflower Kernels
- 1/2 cup PLANTERS Sliced Almonds, toasted

## Instructions

1. Prepare salad dressing mix in small bowl as directed on envelope. Stir in sugar and soy sauce.
2. Break apart noodles; discard seasoning packets from soup mix. Add coleslaw blend, onions, sunflower kernels and almonds to noodles; mix lightly.
3. Add dressing; toss to coat. Serve immediately.
