# Stuffed Eggs

**Servings:** 2 dozen

## Ingredients

- 12 hard-cooked eggs
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1/4 cup sweet pickle relish, drained
- 1 tablespoon honey mustard
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- 1/4 teaspoon pepper
- Fresh parsley sprigs, chopped, optional

## Instructions

1. Slice eggs in half lengthwise; remove yolks and set whites aside.
2. In a small bowl, mash yolks with a fork.
3. Add the mayonnaise, pickle relish, honey mustard, garlic powder, salt, Worcestershire sauce and pepper; mix well.
4. Stuff or pipe filling into the egg whites.
5. Refrigerate until serving.
6. Garnish with parsley if desired.
