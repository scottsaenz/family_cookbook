# Lazy Cake Cookies

## Ingredients

- 1 box yellow cake mix
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 stick melted butter
- 2 cups chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Mix together all ingredients.
2. Bake in 9x13 pan at 350°F for 20 minutes.

## Notes

Optional: top with icing if desired.
