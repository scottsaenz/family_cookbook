# Time Italian Sausage & Potatoes

## Ingredients

- Italian sausage
- Potatoes
- Onions
- Oil

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Oil pan.
3. Cut up potatoes and onions.
4. Cook for 15 minutes.
5. Add Italian sausage and cook for one hour.
