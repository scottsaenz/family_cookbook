# Stromboli

**Cook:** 15 min

## Ingredients

- Pizza dough
- Pizza sauce or crushed tomato sauce
- Pepperoni
- Fresh basil
- Mozzarella cheese

## Instructions

1. Roll pizza dough.
2. Put sauce, pepperoni, basil, and mozzarella cheese on dough.
3. Roll up dough.
4. Put dough on baking sheet.
5. Brush with olive oil.
6. Bake at 375°F for 15 minutes.
