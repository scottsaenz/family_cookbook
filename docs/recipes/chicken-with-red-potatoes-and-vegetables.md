# Chicken with Red Potatoes and Vegetables

**Cook:** 1 hour

## Ingredients

- 2 chicken breasts
- Baking potatoes
- Broccoli
- Chicken seasoning
- 1/2 cup shredded cheese
- 1 stick melted butter

## Instructions

1. Place, mix, and season chicken, broccoli, and potatoes in a greased 9x13 pan.
2. Sprinkle with shredded cheese.
3. Drizzle melted butter on top.
4. Bake for 1 hour at 350°F.
