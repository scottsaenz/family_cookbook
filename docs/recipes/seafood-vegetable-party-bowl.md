# Seafood Vegetable Party Bowl

## Ingredients

- 1 jar artichokes
- 2 cans black olives
- 3 jars whole mushrooms
- 2 containers grape tomatoes
- 1 small container feta cheese
- 1 lb medium shrimp
- 2 packages crab meat
- 2 bottles Italian dressing
- garlic salt
- salt
- pepper

## Instructions

1. Mix all ingredients together.
