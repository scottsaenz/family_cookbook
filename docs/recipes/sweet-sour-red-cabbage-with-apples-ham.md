# Sweet & Sour Red Cabbage With Apples & Ham

**Servings:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 jar (16 oz.) Aunt Nellie's Sweet & Sour Red Cabbage
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 medium Granny Smith apples, unpeeled, cut into 1/2-inch thick wedges
- 1 cup diced cooked ham
- 3 tbsp packed light brown sugar
- 1/4 tsp ground allspice or cinnamon

## Instructions

1. Drain cabbage.
2. Melt butter in large skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Add apples and ham; cook 1 min, stirring occasionally.
4. Stir in brown sugar and allspice; cook and stir 2 min.
5. Add cabbage; cook until heated through, about 2 min, stirring occasionally.

## Notes

Variation: Omit ham for meatless dish.
