# Lasagna Recipe

## Ingredients

- 4 lasagna noodles
- 5 cups noodles
- 6 slices provolone
- 2 cups cottage cheese mixture
- 1 cup mozzarella
- 1 cup mozzarella
- Cottage cheese mixture:
- 3 cups small curd cottage cheese
- 8 ounces ricotta cheese
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 3 tablespoons parsley
- 3 eggs

## Instructions

1. 13 x 8 pan.
2. Layer 4 lasagna noodles.
3. Add 5 cups noodles and 6 slices provolone.
4. Add 2 cups cottage cheese mixture.
5. Add 1 cup mozzarella.
6. Add 3 more noodles.
7. Add sauce and 1 cup cottage cheese mixture.
8. Top with remaining noodles.
9. Melt sauce and mozzarella.
10. Cover and add 3 tablespoons for 20 minutes.
11. Cool and remove from oven.
12. Bake for 20 minutes.
13. Let sit for 15 minutes.
14. Mix cottage cheese mixture ingredients together.
