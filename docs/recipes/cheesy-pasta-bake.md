# Cheesy Pasta Bake

**Prep:** 15 minutes | **Total:** 40 minutes | **Servings:** 8 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 (15-oz) jar Publix Premium Creamy Alfredo Sauce
- 2 cups Sargento Fancy Shredded Sharp Cheddar Cheese, divided
- 1 cup Sargento Fancy Shredded Swiss Cheese
- 1/4 cup Publix Crumbled Blue Cheese
- 1 cup whole milk
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon pepper
- 8-oz penne pasta (3 cups)

## Instructions

1. Bring water to a boil for pasta.
2. Preheat oven to 400°F.
3. Combine in large bowl: Alfredo sauce, 1 cup Cheddar cheese, Swiss cheese, blue cheese, milk, salt, and pepper; set aside.
4. Cook pasta following package instructions; drain. Stir into cheese sauce until well blended.
5. Transfer to 2-quart baking dish; top with remaining 1 cup Cheddar cheese.
6. Bake 20-25 minutes or until hot and bubbly.
