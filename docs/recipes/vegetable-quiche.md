# Vegetable Quiche

**Cook:** 45 min

## Ingredients

- 1 cup sliced fresh mushrooms
- 1 cup each chopped onions and fresh broccoli
- 5 eggs
- 2/3 cup milk
- 1/4 cup chopped roasted red peppers
- 1 cup KRAFT Shredded Three Cheese with a Touch of PHILADELPHIA
- 1 frozen deep-dish pie crust (9 inch), unbaked

## Instructions

1. HEAT oven to 375°F.
2. COOK mushrooms, onions and broccoli in skillet sprayed with cooking spray on medium heat 5 min. or until crisp-tender, stirring occasionally. Remove from heat.
3. BEAT eggs and milk in medium bowl with whisk until well blended. Stir in vegetable mixture, peppers and 1/2 cup cheese; pour into pie crust. Place on baking sheet.
4. BAKE 35 min. Top with remaining cheese; bake 5 to 10 min. or until knife inserted in center comes out clean. Let stand 10 min. before serving.

## Notes

Nutrition information per serving: 290 calories, 17g total fat, 8g saturated fat, 350mg sodium, 22g carbohydrate, 13g protein.
