# Creamy Cabbage

## Ingredients

- 2 TBS butter
- 4 cups shredded cabbage
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1/3 cup evaporated milk

## Instructions

1. Heat 2 TBS butter.
2. Add 4 cups shredded cabbage and stir to blend.
3. Cover and simmer for 5 minutes.
4. Add salt and pepper, 1/3 cup evaporated milk.
5. Cook 4 minutes longer.

## Notes

Do not overcook.
