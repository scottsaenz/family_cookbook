# Gingered Broccoli with Almonds

**Cook:** 2 1/2 - 3 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 cups fresh broccoli florets
- 2 tablespoons slivered almonds
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 3/4 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1/4 teaspoon salt

## Instructions

1. Seal Ziploc® Zip 'n Steam™ bag and shake to mix ingredients. Place bag in microwave.
2. Cook on full power for 2 1/2 - 3 minutes. Use shorter time for a crisper, yet tender result.
3. Allow bag to stand 1 minute before handling. Gently shake bag to distribute butter and seasonings. Carefully open bag and serve.
