# Blue Fettuccine

## Ingredients

- 1 stick butter
- garlic
- 1 med heavy whipping cream
- crab meat

## Instructions

1. Melt butter.
2. Sauté garlic.
3. Add cream - stir until thick.
4. Add crab meat.
