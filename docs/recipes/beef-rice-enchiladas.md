# Beef & Rice Enchiladas

## Ingredients

- 1 box rice a roni spanish rice
- 1 can diced tomatoes with chilis
- 8 flour tortillas
- 4 cups shredded cheese
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 can enchilada sauce

## Instructions

1. Brown meat and add rice.
2. Put enchilada sauce in bottom of pan.
3. Put mixture on tortillas.
4. Fill with cheese with some cheese on top.
