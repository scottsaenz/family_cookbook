# Chicken with Ricotta Cheese

**Cook:** 25-30 min

## Ingredients

- Ricotta cheese
- Fresh spinach
- Chicken breast
- Salt
- Pepper
- Mozzarella cheese
- Tomato sauce

## Instructions

1. Sauté spinach, add cheese.
2. Slice chicken breast, put cheese mixture in slices.
3. Salt and pepper, then add mozzarella cheese.
4. Bake for 25-30 minutes at 400 degrees.
5. Add tomato sauce.
