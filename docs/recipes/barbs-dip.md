# Barb's Dip

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Hellmann's mayo
- 1 cup sour cream
- dill weed (mild)
- garlic salt or garlic powder
- parsley, dry
- if using garlic powder add seasoned salt
- garlic seasoning blend
- celery salt
