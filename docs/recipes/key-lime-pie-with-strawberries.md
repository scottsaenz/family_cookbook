# Key Lime Pie with Strawberries

## Ingredients

- 1 can condensed milk
- 1 cup lime juice
- 4 eggs
- 3/4 cup sugar
- strawberries

## Instructions

1. Mix lime juice, sugar, and eggs.
2. Pour into pie shell.
3. Cook until firm, approximately 15 minutes.
4. Take out and top with strawberries.
5. Make glaze with apricot jam and water.
6. Cook.
