# Chicken Pasta Salad

## Ingredients

- 1 lb cooked bow-tie pasta, cooled
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 lb chicken breast, diced
- 1/2 onion, sliced into half moons
- 1/2 c raisins
- 1 c Hellmann's Mayonnaise Dressing with Olive Oil
- 1/2 c orange juice (or more to taste)
- 3/4 c green olives, pitted and halved

## Instructions

1. Add oil, chicken, onions, and raisins to a hot pan; cook until chicken is cooked through and onions are softened. Set aside to cool.
2. Combine Hellmann's and orange juice to create a dressing; add salt and pepper to taste.
3. Toss with olives, pasta, and chicken mixture.

## Notes

Also delicious with Hellmann's Real Mayonnaise!
