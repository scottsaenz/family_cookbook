# Nancy Potatoes

## Ingredients

- 5 medium red potatoes
- 1/4 cup mayo
- 1 tablespoon grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 green onion, sliced
- 2 teaspoons cider vinegar
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon pepper

## Instructions

1. Cook potatoes.
2. Mix other stuff together.
3. Serve.
