# Baked Mini Omelettes

**Cook:** 20-25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 8 eggs
- diced onions
- ham, bacon, etc.
- 1/4 cup milk
- 1/2 tsp oil
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1 cup grated cheese
- salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. Sauté onions in a pan.
3. Put in greased muffin pans.
4. Add cheese.
5. Pour in egg mixture.
6. Bake for 20-25 minutes.
