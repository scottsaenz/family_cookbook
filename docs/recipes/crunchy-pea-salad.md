# Crunchy Pea Salad

**Servings:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 (15-oz) package frozen green peas
- 1 cup dry-roasted, salted peanuts
- 1 cup dried sweetened cranberries
- 1/2 cup cooked bacon pieces
- 1/3 cup Vidalia onion salad dressing
- 1/4 cup presliced green onions
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon pepper

## Instructions

1. Microwave peas on HIGH for 2 minutes or until thawed.
2. Combine remaining ingredients in salad bowl. Add peas; toss to coat. Chill until ready to serve.
