# Tuna Cakes

**Prep:** 10 min | **Total:** 26 min | **Servings:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- 2 cans (5 oz. each) light tuna in water, drained, flaked
- 1 pkg. (6 oz.) Stove Top Stuffing Mix for Chicken
- 1 cup Kraft 2% Milk Shredded Cheddar Cheese
- 3/4 cup water
- 1 carrot, shredded
- 1/3 cup Miracle Whip Dressing
- 2 Tbsp. Claussen Sweet Pickle Relish

## Instructions

1. Mix all ingredients. Refrigerate 10 min.
2. Heat large nonstick skillet sprayed with cooking spray on medium heat. Add 1/4-cup portions of tuna mixture, in batches, to skillet.
3. Flatten into patties with back of spatula. Cook 3 min. on each side or until golden brown.

## Notes

Serving suggestion: Serve with hot cooked corn and a tossed green salad to round out the meal.
