# Cucumbers and Tomatoes

## Ingredients

- 1 cucumber (rinsed)
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes (rinsed)
- 1/2 cup pre-diced onions
- 1/2 cup Kraft Special Collection Greek Vinaigrette
- 1/2 teaspoon Greek seasoning
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- cilantro fresh
- parsley fresh

## Instructions

1. Remove ends and peel, if desired, from cucumber. Cut cucumber in half lengthwise; then slice thinly and place in medium bowl.
2. Cut cherry tomatoes lengthwise into quarters; add to cucumbers. Stir in remaining ingredients until evenly coated. Let stand 10–15 minutes to blend flavors. Stir and serve.
