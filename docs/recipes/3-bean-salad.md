# 3 Bean Salad

## Ingredients

- 1/3 cup cider vinegar
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1/2 cup parsley
- 1 tablespoon rosemary
- 1/2 cup diced onion
- 1 can green beans
- 1 can kidney beans
- 1 can garbanzo beans

## Instructions

1. Whisk together cider vinegar, sugar, and olive oil.
2. Add parsley, rosemary, diced onion, green beans, kidney beans, and garbanzo beans.
3. Toss together and refrigerate.
