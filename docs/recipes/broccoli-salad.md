# Broccoli Salad

## Ingredients

- Red onion, sliced very thin
- 3 or 4 heads of broccoli
- Yellow raisins
- Sunflower seeds
- 1 or 2 pkg real bacon pieces
- 3 cups Hellmann's mayo
- 6 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 3/4 cup sugar

## Instructions

1. Combine sliced red onion, broccoli, yellow raisins, sunflower seeds, and bacon pieces in a large bowl.
2. In a separate bowl, mix Hellmann's mayo, red wine vinegar, and sugar until well combined.
3. Pour the dressing over the broccoli mixture and toss to coat evenly.
4. Chill in the refrigerator before serving.

## Notes

Adjust the amount of red onion and bacon pieces to taste.
