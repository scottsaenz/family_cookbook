# Bisquick, Sour Cream, 7up + Butter Biscuits

**Cook:** 12-15 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 cups Bisquick
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 cup 7up
- 1/2 cup melted butter

## Instructions

1. Mix Bisquick, sour cream, and 7up. Dough will be soft.
2. Knead and fold dough with baking mix.
3. Pat dough out and cut biscuits using a round biscuit/cookie cutter.
4. Melt butter in bottom of cookie sheet pan or 9x13 casserole dish.
5. Place biscuits on top of melted butter and bake at 450 degrees for 12-15 minutes or until brown.
