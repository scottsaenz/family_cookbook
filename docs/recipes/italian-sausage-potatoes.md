# Italian Sausage & Potatoes

## Ingredients

- 4 potatoes
- 1 onion
- Italian sausage

## Instructions

1. Spray pan.
2. Cube potatoes and chop onions.
3. Add butter.
4. Bake for 15 minutes.
5. Add sausage.
6. Bake 1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes uncovered.
