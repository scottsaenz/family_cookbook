# Chicken Breast with Provolone Cheese & Asparagus

**Cook:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 6 chicken breasts
- Provolone cheese
- Asparagus spears
- Italian dressing
- Bread crumbs

## Instructions

1. Flatten chicken breast.
2. Put Provolone cheese and asparagus spears - about 4 spears.
3. Spritz Italian dressing & sprinkle bread crumbs.
4. Bake for 20 minutes.
