# Kaya Turkey Recipe

## Ingredients

- onion
- yellow crooked neck squash
- small zucchini
- sliced mushrooms
- salt & pepper
- 1 can mushroom soup
- red pepper
- yellow pepper
- orange
- 1/2 cup or more Italian dressing

## Instructions

1. Sauté onion, yellow crooked neck squash, small zucchini, sliced mushrooms, salt & pepper.
2. Brown turkey meat and chop until fine. When done, add 1 can mushroom soup and stir until mixed. Mix together.
3. Sauté onion, red pepper, yellow pepper, orange, sliced mushrooms, salt & pepper.
4. Use 1/2 cup or more Italian dressing to meat mixture instead of mushroom soup.
