# Chicken Casserole

## Ingredients

- Butter
- Flour
- Salt
- Pepper
- Chicken breast
- Bisquick
- Milk
- Chicken soup
- 2 cups of [unclear]

## Instructions

1. Melt butter in bottom of pan.
2. Sprinkle flour, salt, and pepper.
3. Mix in small bowl Bisquick and milk.
4. Pour slowly over chicken. Don't stir.
5. In a mixing bowl, mix chicken soup and 2 cups of [unclear].
6. Pour over chicken.

## Notes

Very tasty.
