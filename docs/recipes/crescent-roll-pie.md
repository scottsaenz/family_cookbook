# Crescent Roll Pie

**Cook:** 15 min

## Ingredients

- 2 tubes crescent rolls
- 1 can pie filling
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 2 TBS milk

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 325°F.
2. Spray muffin tins.
3. Lay crescent roll flat side down.
4. In tin, fill 2 TBS filling.
5. Fold over.
6. Bake for 15 min.
