# Bev's Bean Soup

## Ingredients

- Ham
- Carrots
- Onion
- 1 diced potato
- Navy beans
- Small can tomato sauce

## Instructions

1. Boil ham until ham comes off bone.
2. Cut up carrots, onion, and 1 diced potato.
3. Add navy beans and small can of tomato sauce.
