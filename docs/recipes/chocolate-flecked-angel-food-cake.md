# Chocolate Flecked Angel Food Cake

**Cook:** 30-42 minutes | **Servings:** 12 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 package (16 oz.) Angel Food Cake mix
- 1 1/4 cups water
- 1 package (6 oz.) semi-sweet chocolate morsels
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Prepare cake mix according to package directions. Pour batter into ungreased 10-inch tube pan.
3. Bake 30-42 minutes or until top is golden brown and cracks feel dry and not sticky.
4. Invert pan on heatproof funnel or bottle until completely cool.
5. In a bowl, toss chocolate morsels with cornstarch to coat.
6. Fold morsels into cooled cake.
7. Remove cake from pan.

## Notes

Makes about 12 servings. Dust cooled cake with powdered sugar if desired.
