# Lime Jello Salad

## Ingredients

- 1 large box lime jello
- 2 cups water, boiling
- pineapple juice, drained
- cream cheese
- pineapples
- cool whip

## Instructions

1. Dissolve lime jello in 2 cups boiling water.
2. Drain pineapple juice and add to jello.
3. Mash cream cheese into jello.
4. Add pineapples.
5. Whisk in cool whip.
6. Mix all together.
