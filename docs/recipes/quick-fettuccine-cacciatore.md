# Quick Fettuccine Cacciatore

**Prep:** 5 min | **Total:** 20 min | **Servings:** 4 servings, 1 1/4 cups each

## Ingredients

- 8 oz. fettuccine, uncooked
- 1 lb. boneless skinless chicken breasts, cut into bite-size pieces
- 1 cup each green pepper strips and sliced mushrooms
- 1 can (14 1/2 oz.) diced tomatoes, undrained
- 1/4 cup Kraft Zesty Italian Dressing
- 1/2 cup Kraft Shredded Italian Mozzarella Parmesan Cheese Blend
- 1/2 cup chopped fresh basil leaves

## Instructions

1. Cook pasta as directed on package.
2. Meanwhile, heat oil in large nonstick skillet on medium-high heat. Add chicken, cook 3 min. or until no longer pink.
3. Add peppers and mushrooms, cook 3 min. stirring occasionally.
4. Stir in tomatoes and dressing. Reduce heat to medium-low, simmer 5 min. or until chicken is cooked through.
5. Drain pasta. Toss with chicken mixture. Sprinkle with cheese and basil.
