# Shrimp Bisque

## Ingredients

- Butter
- Chopped onion
- Pepper
- Garlic
- Cream
- Shrimp
- Sherry
- Mushroom soup
- Tomato soup

## Instructions

1. Melt butter.
2. Add chopped onion, pepper, and garlic.
3. Add cream, shrimp, sherry, mushroom soup, and tomato soup.
4. Season to taste.
